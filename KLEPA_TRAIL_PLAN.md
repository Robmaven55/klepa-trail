## 🚗 PHASE 1 — DRIVING: SKOPJE → MONASTERY (08:00 departure)

### Route for 50 People in Cars

**🗺️ Google Maps Link (Driving Route):**  
[Click to open in Google Maps — Skopje to Monastery Archangel Michael, Gorno Chichevo](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Skopje/41.5637,21.8656)

**Step-by-step Driving Directions:**
1. 🚗 **Depart Skopje** → Head south on **A1 / E75 motorway** (direction: Veles / Gevgelija)
2. 🚗 Pass through **Veles** — continue south ~20km
3. 🚗 Take the **Gradsko exit** off the A1 motorway
4. 🚗 In Gradsko, follow signs toward **Dolno Chichevo**
5. 🚗 Drive through **Dolno Chichevo** → continue upward to **Gorno Chichevo**
6. 🚗 From Gorno Chichevo, follow the **dirt road (last 2km)** to the **Monastery Archangel Michael**

**⚠️ IMPORTANT DRIVING NOTES:**
- Total distance: ~80 km from Skopje
- Estimated driving time: **1 hour 15 min** (plus 15 min for final dirt road)
- The last **2 km is an unpaved dirt road** — SUVs/4x4 recommended or careful driving
- Road from Gradsko is **narrow** — convoy driving recommended, not overtaking
- Depart Skopje no later than **06:30–06:45** to arrive by 08:00

**🅿️ Parking at the Monastery:**
- Park in the **open area/field near the monastery grounds** (no formal lot)
- 50 people in cars ≈ 12–15 vehicles
- Organize cars in **two rows** to maximize space
- Assign a parking coordinator to guide vehicles in
- **DO NOT block the monastery gate or entrance path**

## ⛪ PHASE 2 — MONASTERY BASE CAMP (08:00 – 09:00)

### On Arrival at Monastery Archangel Michael

**08:00 — Arrival & Settling**
- Welcome everyone at the monastery courtyard
- Head count: confirm all 50 participants are present
- Restroom break (monastery facilities)
- Distribute **lunch packets** to each of the 50 hikers
- Distribute **water bottles** (minimum 1.5L per person)

**08:15 — Chef Team Briefing (Stays at Monastery)**
- The kitchen/chef team **does NOT hike** — they stay at the monastery
- Chefs begin preparing the **hot group lunch** for the afternoon return (~15:00)
- Suggested menu: traditional Macedonian dishes (tavče gravče, ajvar, grilled meat, bread)
- Lunch should be ready and served by **15:15–15:30**

**08:30 — Orientation & Briefing for Hikers**

> *Give this speech/explanation to the group:*

---
### 📣 GROUP BRIEFING SCRIPT

**"Welcome everyone to the Klepa Trail!**

You are standing in front of the Monastery of St. Archangel Michael, built in 1861 by the master builder Andreja Damjanov — one of the most celebrated church builders in the Balkans. This monastery sits at the heart of the Klepa ethnographic region, one of the most historically rich and scenically dramatic landscapes in North Macedonia.

Today we will hike from here (~600m elevation) to the peak of Mount Klepa at **1,150 meters above sea level** — a climb of approximately **550 meters** over **9 kilometers** one-way.

Our route takes us through the completely **abandoned village of Dvorishte** — once a thriving Macedonian community, today a fascinating ghost village of stone ruins. We will pause there and reflect on its history.

At the peak, you will be rewarded with **panoramic views** of the entire Tikveš region, the Vardar-Crna river confluence, and even the ancient archaeological site of Stobi.

Please listen to the safety guidelines now..."**

---

## 🏕️ PAUSE 1 — DVORISHTE GHOST VILLAGE (~10:00 | 800m | km 3.5)

**What to do here (30 minutes):**
- Rest, eat a snack, drink water
- **Photo opportunity** — dramatic ruins of the old Macedonian stone houses
- Group leader gives a **short historical explanation:**

> *"Dvorishte was a Macedonian village on the eastern slopes of Mount Klepa. By the early 20th century, its population had emigrated in search of better conditions, and today not a single permanent resident remains. These stone walls are all that is left. As you walk through, imagine the families that once lived here..."*

- ⚠️ Warn hikers: **don't enter unstable structures**
- Head count before moving on

---

## 🌅 PAUSE 2 — PANORAMA VIEWPOINT (~11:15 | 950m | km 6)

**What to do here (20 minutes):**
- Rest, hydrate, eat from lunch packet if needed
- **360° panoramic views** begin here — excellent photography spot
- Visible landmarks:
  - 🏛️ **Stobi archaeological site** in the valley below
  - 🌊 **Vardar-Crna river confluence**
  - 🗻 **Tikveš wine region** spreading south
- Group leader points out landmarks
- **Mandatory group photo here** with the valley in background

---

## 🪖 PAUSE 3 — BUNKER RIDGE (~12:15 | 1050m | km 8)

**What to do here (15 minutes):**
- Short final rest before summit
- **Explore abandoned WWII/Yugoslav military bunkers** at this ridgeline
- Group leader gives context:

> *"These bunkers were built during Yugoslavia's military era. The strategic position of Klepa made it a key defensive location — from here you could monitor the entire central Vardar valley. Today they are a fascinating historical remnant."*

- Final water check — summit is only 15 min away
- Motivational moment — group can see the **tooth-shaped rock at the summit**

---

## 🏆 SUMMIT — KLEPA PEAK (12:30 | 1150m)

**At the Peak (30 minutes):**
- 🎉 Celebrate! Group photo at the summit rock
- Enjoy the views — panorama of all of central North Macedonia
- The summit features a **dramatic volcanic neck rock formation**
- Distribute remaining snacks / last water check
- **13:00 — Begin descent**

---

## 🔽 PHASE 4 — DESCENT: KLEPA PEAK → MONASTERY (13:00 – 15:00)

- Same route in reverse
- Descent is faster but requires **careful footing** on rocky sections
- Estimated 2 hours descent
- Arrive monastery: **~15:00**

---

## 🍽️ PHASE 5 — LUNCH AT THE MONASTERY (15:00 – 16:30)

- Chefs will have **hot lunch ready** for the group
- Traditional Macedonian feast in the monastery courtyard
- Suggested dishes:
  - Tavče gravče (baked beans)
  - Grilled meats (kebapčinja, kobasici)
  - Fresh salads (šopska salata)
  - Ajvar & bread
  - Dessert: baklava or tulumbi
  - Drinks: rakija (for adults), water, juices
- After lunch: **free time** to explore the monastery church & grounds
- Guided monastery tour available (arrange with monk/priest in advance)

---

## 🚗 PHASE 6 — RETURN DRIVE TO SKOPJE (~16:30 departure)

- Same route in reverse
- Estimated return: **~18:00 in Skopje**
- Recommend a **convoy drive** back until Gradsko, then free driving on A1
