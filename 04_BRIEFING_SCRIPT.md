# Morning Briefing Script — 08:15 at the Monastery
*Speak slowly. Repeat the key safety items twice.*

---

## 1. Welcome (1 min)

> "Good morning. We are 50 people today. We're standing at the **Monastery of Sv. Arhangel Mihail**, around 600 meters above sea level. This monastery was built between **1695 and 1705** — older than most cities in this region — by a monk named Michael with the help of a local protector from Veles. Two churches: St. Archangel Michael and St. Mother of God."

## 2. The plan in 60 seconds (1 min)

> "We hike from here through the abandoned village of **Dvorishte** — a place that had 34 people in 1961 and zero since 1981. Then we climb to **Klepa Peak — 1,150 meters**. That's the volcanic rock that looks like a tooth above us — you can see it from here. We pause three times. We reach the summit around 13:00. We come back the same way. We are back here at the monastery at 15:00, and we eat a proper lunch that the chefs have been cooking since this morning."

## 3. The three pauses (2 min)

> "**Pause 1 — Dvorishte, the abandoned village.** Forty minutes in. We stop fifteen minutes. We take photos. We don't enter the houses — they collapse.
>
> **Pause 2 — the saddle, around 900 meters.** Two hours in. Fifteen minutes. You see Tikvesh lake to the south, the Vardar valley to the east, Stobi the ancient city — all from one spot.
>
> **Pause 3 — base of the summit rock.** This is where the trail gets steep — a real scramble over volcanic rock. If you don't want to scramble the last hundred meters, you wait here with one of the guides. No judgment. It's a real scramble."

## 4. Safety — non-negotiable (3 min — say it twice)

> "Three rules.
>
> **One — stay between the front guide and the back guide.** If you're behind the back guide, you are lost. Don't go ahead of the front guide. We move as one group.
>
> **Two — drink water before you are thirsty.** You should be sipping every fifteen minutes. We do not have a water source on this trail. If you run out, tell the guide immediately.
>
> **Three — at every pause, we count. We count at the start, at every pause, at the summit, on the way down. If you need to step off the trail for any reason — tell someone next to you. Don't disappear silently.**
>
> If you twist an ankle, you stop and you tell the nearest person. Don't be brave. We have first-aid with both guides.
>
> Emergency number in this country is **112**. Mountain rescue: **+389 2 3138 142**. These numbers are printed on your lunch bag."

## 5. Lunch packets (1 min)

> "Each of you gets a lunch packet now. Your name is on it. Put it in your backpack. Don't eat it before Pause 2 — you'll be too full to climb. Save the chocolate for the summit. There's a small water bottle inside, but you should also have your own 1.5 liter bottle — show it to the guide if you don't.
>
> When we get back at 15:00, the chefs have the real lunch ready. Tavče gravče, salad, meat — proper meal."

## 6. Gear check — visible (2 min)

> "Now I look at every one of you.
>
> Show me your boots. Are they hiking boots? No sneakers? Good.
>
> Show me your water bottle. At least one and a half liters? Good.
>
> Show me your jacket — even a thin one. The summit is windy.
>
> Show me your hat. Sunscreen on now if you haven't already.
>
> Phone charged? Show me 80% or more.
>
> If any of these are missing — come to me now, before we start. Not on the trail."

## 7. The story behind the day (1 min — optional, depends on group)

> "Klepa is a volcanic neck — that means a hard plug of magma that survived after the softer mountain around it eroded away. Five hundred million years of geology and you climb to the top of it in three hours. Last year, five hundred people climbed it in one day in honor of two friends who died in the mountains. We are fifty. We climb for the day, for each other, and we come back here for a real meal. Let's go."

## 8. Start the hike — 09:00

> "Lead guide front. Sweep guide back. Single file on the narrow sections. Let's move."

---

## Notes for the speaker
- Total briefing time: **~10 minutes**. Don't go longer — 50 people lose focus.
- Repeat the three safety rules at every pause.
- If anyone arrives late (after 08:30), they don't hike. Non-negotiable.
- Keep one printed copy of the headcount list on a clipboard. Yes, paper.
