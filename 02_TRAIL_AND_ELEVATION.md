# Trail & Elevation — Monastery → Dvorishte → Klepa Peak

## Trail overview
- **Length:** ~6 km one way (12 km round trip)
- **Elevation gain:** ~690 m total (descend ~140 m to Dvorishte, then climb ~690 m to peak)
- **Difficulty:** 8/10 — terrain is loose volcanic rock; the final 200 m to the summit is a scramble around the "tooth" rock
- **Ascent time:** ~3 h 15 min for a 50-person group (pace of slowest hiker)
- **Descent time:** ~1 h 45 min
- **Total moving time + pauses:** ~5 h 30 min

## Three pause points (researched & rationale)

### Pause 1 — DVORISHTE (the abandoned village)
- **Elevation:** 459 m · **Coordinates:** 41.57833° N, 21.83611° E
- **Distance from start:** ~1.5 km (~30–40 min hiking)
- **Stop duration:** 15–20 min
- **Why here:** First natural break after the descent. Strong photo and story stop — the village had 34 residents in 1961, 2 in 1981, and zero since. Pure Macedonian Christian village, abandoned during the rural exodus to Veles. The "Kale" archaeological site nearby dates to late antiquity. This is the most emotionally resonant point on the trail.
- **What to do:** Group photo against ruined houses. Quick narration of the history. Water top-up. Re-tighten boots before the climb.
- **Caution:** Don't enter ruined structures — collapse risk.

### Pause 2 — RIDGE SADDLE (Tikvesh viewpoint)
- **Elevation:** ~900 m · **Distance from start:** ~4.0 km (~2 h hiking from Dvorishte)
- **Stop duration:** 10–15 min
- **Why here:** This is the natural geographic plateau before the final steep section. From here you see south to the **Tikvesh basin and lake**, east to the **middle Vardar** and the ancient city of **Stobi**, northwest to **Solunska Glava** (the high peak across the Azot valley). Klepa is a *volcanic neck* with strategic visibility — this is the first viewpoint that proves it.
- **What to do:** Snack from lunch packet. Reapply sunscreen (the next section is exposed). Headcount + check all 50 are present before the scramble.

### Pause 3 — SUMMIT BASE (below the tooth rock)
- **Elevation:** ~1,050 m · **Distance from start:** ~5.5 km
- **Stop duration:** 10 min
- **Why here:** The large rock at the summit looks "like a tooth" — and the final ~100 m of elevation is a near-vertical scramble over volcanic rock around old abandoned military bunkers. Some of the 50 may not want to scramble the last bit. **This is the decision pause.** Anyone uncomfortable waits here with a designated guide.
- **What to do:** Brief safety reminder. Anyone summitting goes in groups of 5–8 max. The peak top is small — don't crowd it.

## Summit — Klepa Peak (1,150 m)
- **View:** 360° — Vardar valley + Stobi + Tikvesh + Solunska Glava + Babuna
- **Time at top:** 20–25 min for photos, group shot, lunch-packet eaten if anyone is hungry
- **Caution:** The abandoned military bunkers under the rock are interesting but unstable — look, don't enter.

## Descent
- Same route back. Faster (1 h 45 min) but **knees and ankles take the punishment.**
- Quick water-stop at Dvorishte on the way back (5 min, no full pause).
- Arrive monastery ~15:00.

## Gear checklist (tell every hiker in advance)
- **Boots** — hiking boots or sturdy trail runners. No sneakers, no sandals.
- **Water** — minimum **1.5 L per person**. No reliable water source on the trail.
- **Layers** — light jacket. Wind on the summit even on warm days.
- **Sunscreen + hat** — sections are fully exposed.
- **Snack** — even though lunch packets are provided, an extra energy bar/banana is wise.
- **Phone with offline map** — download the trail in Maps.me or Komoot before leaving Skopje (signal will drop in the valley).
