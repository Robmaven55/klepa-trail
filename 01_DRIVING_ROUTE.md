## Driving plan — in three legs

### Leg 1 — Skopje → Gradsko exit (A1 motorway)
- **Distance:** ~95 km · **Time:** ~1 h 5 min
- Take **A1 motorway south** out of Skopje toward Veles/Gevgelija
- Stay on A1 through Veles, continue south
- **Take the GRADSKO exit** (signposted; it's a major junction — the A1/A4 split)
- **Fuel/coffee opportunity:** Petrol stations on A1 around Veles or at Gradsko exit

### Leg 2 — Gradsko → Gorno Chichevo village
- **Distance:** ~14 km · **Time:** ~20 min
- After exit, head **west/northwest** toward the village of **Dolno Chichevo**, then continue up to **Gorno Chichevo**
- Asphalt road, narrow but driveable for any passenger car

### Leg 3 — Gorno Chichevo → Monastery (the rough section)
- **Distance:** ~3–5 km · **Time:** ~15–20 min
- Branch off the inter-village road onto the **monastery access road**
- ⚠️ **WARNING — road condition is poor.** Documented as "in bad condition." Tell drivers: keep speed under 20 km/h, watch for potholes and washouts.
- SUVs and crossovers fine. **Low-clearance sedans should drive in second gear with extra following distance** — no rush.
- Pull-out/passing spots exist but are few. **Convoy in single file.**

## Convoy logistics for 50 people

| Roles | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lead car (knows the route) | 1 | Drives 5 km/h slower than feels natural, keeps the convoy together |
| Sweep car (last in line) | 1 | Carries first-aid kit + spare phone + jumper cables |
| Cars in between | ~10–13 | Assume 4 ppl/car average for 50 people |

- **Departure:** 06:00 from a single rally point in Skopje (e.g., Ramstor parking, Bunjakovec, or Aerodrom — pick one).
- **Rally point parking is more important than the route** — easier to lose one car at the start than at km 90.
- **Walkie-talkie or WhatsApp live-location** group required. Phones may lose signal in the Chichevo valley.
- **Buffer for stragglers:** Wait 15 min at the Gradsko exit gas station before the final leg. Anyone not arrived by 07:45 calls the sweep car directly.

## Parking at the Monastery

- The monastery yard and the access road shoulder accommodate ~15–20 cars. **For 12+ cars, this fits but is tight.**
- **Designate a "parking marshal"** to direct nose-in parking and keep a turnaround lane clear (50 people will be coming back to these cars after a long hike).
- **Lock everything.** Leave valuables out of sight; the monastery is unattended much of the day.

## Backup
If the rough access road is impassable (snow, mud, washout): park at **Gorno Chichevo village center** and walk the remaining ~3–4 km to the monastery — but this adds an hour and ruins the schedule. Have someone scout the road 2–3 days before.
