## Lunch logistics

### Trail lunch packets (50× — distributed at 08:45)
Each packet should be **lightweight + non-melting + high-calorie** because it rides in a backpack for 4+ hours:
- 1× hearty sandwich (cured meat, cheese, bread that won't go soggy — e.g., bagel or panini)
- 1× hard-boiled egg
- 1× piece of fruit (apple or banana)
- 1× energy/protein bar
- 1× small pack nuts or seeds
- 1× small chocolate
- 1× 0.5 L water (in addition to their own 1.5 L)
- Optional: small electrolyte sachet for the water

**Pack in compostable paper bags labeled with each person's name** — avoids the "whose packet is this" confusion at 11:30 on the trail.

### Return lunch (cooked at the monastery, eaten 15:15)
The monastery kitchen prepares this from ~08:00 onwards. Suggested traditional Macedonian menu:
- **Starter:** Šopska salad + ajvar + bread + sirenje
- **Main:** Tavče gravče OR slow-cooked lamb/beef with kasapsko meso, served with rice or potatoes
- **Side:** Grilled vegetables, mushrooms
- **Drink:** Water, homemade rakija (small), wine, tea
- **Dessert:** Watermelon or seasonal fruit + Turkish coffee

**Confirm with chefs in advance:** dietary restrictions (vegetarian, halal, allergies). Ask all 50 a week before. Plan +10% portion volume — hikers eat more than they expect.

## Safety & risk plan

### Required of every hiker
- Sturdy hiking boots
- 1.5 L water in their own bottle
- Sun hat + sunscreen
- Light rain shell or windbreaker
- Phone charged to 100%

### Carried by the lead + sweep guides
- Full first-aid kit (blister care, sprains, painkillers, antihistamine, eye wash)
- Trauma items (pressure bandage, SAM splint)
- Whistle
- Emergency thermal blankets ×3
- Headlamp (in case of late return)
- Printed list of all 50 names with phone numbers + emergency contacts
- Power bank for phone
- Offline-downloaded GPX of the trail (Komoot / Maps.me / OsmAnd)

### Headcount discipline
- Count at **08:30** (briefing), **09:00** (trail start), **at every pause**, **at the summit**, **at every pause on descent**, **15:00** (back at monastery), **upon convoy departure**.
- Same person counts every time. Write the number down.

### Emergency contacts
- **Macedonia mountain rescue:** **+389 2 3138 142** (Mountain Rescue Service)
- **General emergency:** **112**
- Print these on every lunch-packet bag.

### Weather call
- 48 hours before: check forecast. **Hard cancel** if thunderstorms predicted (volcanic rock + exposed ridge = lightning risk).
- 24 hours before: confirm with group via WhatsApp.

## Communications

- Single WhatsApp group named **"KLEPA TRAIL — DAY OF"**
- One pinned message: today's plan + emergency numbers
- Live location shared by lead car driver from 06:00
- Anyone falling behind on the trail → message the sweep guide directly (don't ping the whole group)
