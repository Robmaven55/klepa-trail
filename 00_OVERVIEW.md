# KLEPA TRAIL — 50-Person Hike
**Date:** TBD · **Group size:** 50 · **Region:** Vardar, North Macedonia

---

## The day in one paragraph
50 people convoy from Skopje at dawn down the **A1 motorway**, exit at **Gradsko**, climb the back road through **Gorno Chichevo** to the **Monastery of Sv. Arhangel Mihail** (~600 m). Cars stay parked at the monastery. Group briefing + lunch-packet handout + short monastery tour. Hike west on the ridge trail through the **abandoned village of Dvorishte** (459 m) and up the volcanic neck of **Klepa Peak** (1,150 m). Three pause points en route. Summit ~13:00, descend, back at monastery by ~15:00 for a sit-down lunch the monastery chefs have been preparing all morning.

## Critical precedents
- A **500-person memorial climb** to Klepa peak (in memory of mountaineers Kalina & Aleksandar) was successfully run on this same mountain — so a 50-person group is well within the trail's documented capacity.
- The Monastery of Sv. Arhangel Mihail was built **1695–1705** by the monk Michael; two churches on site (St. Archangel Michael + St. Mother of God) — strong tour content for the morning briefing.
